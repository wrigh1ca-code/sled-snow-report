Snowfall forecast is built from the high-elevation riding spots in each zone, not the trailhead town. Each spot gets its own forecast at its riding elevation from three weather models (GFS, ECMWF and GEM). The zone number is the average across spots and models. The range under a number shows how far apart the models are; a wide range means low confidence. Models assume fairly dense snow, so cold, dry storms often deliver more than shown.

Snow depth is the latest daily reading from the SNOTEL station that best matches each zone's riding spots. Its elevation and distance are on each row.

Data: USDA NRCS SNOTEL · Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Updated every 3 hours.
